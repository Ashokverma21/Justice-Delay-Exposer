What the model can and cannot do

  • Trained on cases filed/decided 2010-2018 from 15 states. Dates outside that range, and other states, are not supported.
  • Accuracy on held-out cases: R² about 0.71 (log scale) from filing-time information only, about 0.75 when the purpose of listing is also known. This is statistically solid on groups of cases; a single case can easily be off by 2-3x (see the range shown).
  • The model has no knowledge of the actual facts of a case, the judge's workload on that day, or adjournments - only court, case type, act, filing date and similar fields.
  • Cases still pending at the end of 2018 are not in the data, so very long durations are under-represented (right-censoring).
  • This is a research / hackathon tool, not legal advice.
